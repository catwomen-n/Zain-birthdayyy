for every year you've been you ♡
birthday wishes

19 wishes for your 19th birthday 🎂

One little wish for every year of your life.

01

A beautiful year ✦

I hope this new year of your life brings you more happiness than you expect. I hope you wake up excited for what is ahead of you and proud of how far you have already come. You deserve beautiful things, beautiful moments, and people who genuinely love you.

02

Confidence ✦

I hope you learn to see yourself through my eyes sometimes. I wish you could see how capable, special, and worthy you are. Never let one bad day make you question your entire future. You are so much more than your doubts.

03

Your dreams ✦

I hope every dream you have starts feeling a little more possible this year. I want to see you work for the things you want and eventually look back knowing you never gave up. I will always believe that you can accomplish more than you think.

04

Happiness ✦

I wish you the kind of happiness that stays even after the exciting moments are over. I hope you find joy in the smallest things, laugh until your stomach hurts, and have more good days than difficult ones. You deserve to feel genuinely happy.

05

Good people ✦

I hope you are surrounded by people who appreciate your heart and never make you feel like you have to change who you are. I hope you keep the people who bring peace into your life close. And I hope you always know that I am one of the people cheering for you.

06

Peace ✦

I wish you peace on the days when your mind feels too full. I hope you learn that you don't have to solve everything at once. Give yourself the same patience you give to everyone else. You deserve rest, softness, and moments where everything feels okay.

07

Success ✦

I hope all the effort you put into your future eventually comes back to you in ways that make you proud. I want to see you reach goals that once felt impossible. And when you do, I hope you remember how hard you worked to get there.

08

Courage ✦

I wish you the courage to choose what makes you happy, even when it is scary. I hope you never stay somewhere just because you are afraid of change. Trust yourself, trust your instincts, and remember that taking a new step does not mean you are leaving everything behind.

09

Self-belief ✦

Whenever you doubt yourself, I hope you remember that I believe in you. I see things in you that you sometimes forget are there. You don't have to be perfect to be amazing. Just keep becoming the person you want to be.

10

Our memories ✦

I hope this year gives us so many new memories that we stop trying to count them. I want more random conversations, more laughing until we can't breathe, more little moments that only make sense to us. I want to keep collecting pieces of our story with you.

11

Our firsts ✦

I hope we get to experience more of the things distance has made us wait for. The first real hug, the first day together without a screen between us, and all the little firsts that come after. I already know those moments will mean everything to me.

12

Big laughs ✦

I wish you a year filled with the kind of laughter that makes your cheeks hurt. I want to be part of as many of those moments as I possibly can. Some of my favorite memories are simply the moments when we are being stupid together and laughing for no reason.

13

Little moments ✦

I hope you never underestimate the ordinary days. Sometimes the smallest conversations and simplest moments become the memories we remember most. I want a future with you where even doing absolutely nothing together feels special because it is with you.

14

Love ✦

I hope you never question how loved you are. There is someone out here who thinks about you, worries about you, is proud of you, misses you, and loves you more than you probably realize. I hope you carry that feeling with you even on difficult days.

15

Growth ✦

I hope this year helps you become an even stronger and happier version of yourself. Growing doesn't mean becoming someone completely different. It means discovering more of who you already are and becoming comfortable with it. I will be proud of you through every version.

16

Adventure ✦

I hope life surprises you in the best possible ways. I want you to see new places, try new things, meet people who inspire you, and collect stories you will tell for years. And selfishly, I hope I get to be beside you for many of those adventures.

17

Us ✦

I hope we keep choosing each other through every version of ourselves. Relationships aren't about everything always being perfect; they are about continuing to care, communicate, and grow together. I hope we keep building something that feels safe, honest, and completely ours.

18

The future ✦

I hope the future gives you even more than you are brave enough to dream about right now. I hope when you look back at this birthday years from now, you are proud of the person you became. And I hope I am still right there beside you, celebrating you.

19

This birthday ✦

Most of all, I hope you remember how loved you were today. Even though I can't be beside you the way I wish I could, I wanted to make something that reminds you that you are special to me. Happy birthday, my love. I hope this is the beginning of one of your best years yet.
